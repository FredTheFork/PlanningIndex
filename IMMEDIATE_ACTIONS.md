## When Things Go Wrong

**If Google Search Console won't verify:**
- Try "Google Analytics" verification method
- Or try Bing first (often faster)

**If Google My Business is slow to verify:**
- Check mailbox (Google sends physical postcard)
- Or use alternative verification (phone)

**If directories reject your info:**
- Check for typos in phone/email
- Make sure info is EXACTLY consistent
- Some require business registration number (you don't need)
